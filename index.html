About

I'm a systems architect and web designer based in Benton, Arkansas. I recently completed a role at Nanobox in Dublin, where I built a fully offline AI research intelligence platform from the ground up. The system processed peer-reviewed scientific papers, stored them in a vector database, and provided a conversational chatbot for synthesis and citation. Every piece of data stayed inside the organisation.

Before that, I worked with the Arkansas Department of Health on data validation and client onboarding across hospitals and clinics statewide. I also taught cybersecurity to high school students as a GenCyber guest lecturer at UALR.

On the design side, I build projects that explore specific problems: e-commerce with warm brutalism, typography-first editorial layouts, data visualization with D3, and generative audio. I care about restraint, intention, and craft. Every interaction should feel earned, every layout should breathe.

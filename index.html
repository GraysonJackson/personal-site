About

I'm a designer who codes and a developer who cares about design. My work sits at the intersection — I think in systems, build in code, and obsess over details that most people never notice.

After spending time building infrastructure and data pipelines, I shifted focus toward design-forward web projects. Each piece in this portfolio targets a specific design problem: e-commerce, editorial, data visualization, motion design, and personal expression.

I care about restraint, intention, and craft. Every interaction should feel earned, every layout should breathe, and every color choice should have a reason behind it.

Tools

Figma, VS Code, Git, Next.js, React, D3.js, GSAP, Astro, Tailwind

Focus

UI/UX Design, Frontend Development, Creative Coding, Design Systems

Process

How I approach design problems.

01

Understand

Every project starts with a constraint. What's the design problem? What am I trying to prove? What should a reviewer take away?

02

Choose

Stack, visual direction, and interaction model get decided early. I pick tools that serve the design, not the other way around.

03

Build

Iterate quickly. I prototype interactions before polishing visuals. If it doesn't feel right in wireframe form, high fidelity won't save it.

04

Refine

The difference between good and great is in the last 10% — transition timing, hover states, empty states, edge cases. I ship when I'd be proud to show it.
